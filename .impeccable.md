## Design Context

### Users
Focusmate is for people who need help turning vague or stalled work into one concrete next action. They may be using it while distracted, avoidant, tired, or overloaded, so the interface should reduce decisions and keep the active task visible without feeling clinical.

### Brand Personality
Plain, steady, and non-judgmental. The product should feel like a calm workbench: useful, close at hand, and free of motivational noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use one calm focus workbench instead of route-based theme iterations. The interface should keep task, next step, recovery controls, energy, conversation, and parked side tasks visible without becoming a dashboard. Avoid fake metrics, glass effects, glow, gradient text, and decorative panels.

### Design Principles
- Keep the next action visible and readable.
- Prefer one polished working posture over theme swaps.
- Use calm muted colors with one restrained action color.
- Keep controls familiar, compact, and predictable.
- Let empty space support focus instead of filling it with decorative content.
